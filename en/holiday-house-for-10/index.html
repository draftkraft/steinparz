THE WHOLE HOUSE FOR YOUR GROUP

Room for 10.
Just for you.

Ten people, one house: you rent the entire Teichhof with 7 rooms, 3 bathrooms and 300 m². A self-catering group house on its own grounds at the edge of the woods, with no other guests in the house or on the grounds.

FOR GROUPS WITH A SHARED PLAN

The whole group.
One place together.

Questions about Teichhof

What does the house cost?

The whole house costs from €413 per night, depending on the dates, with a minimum stay from 3 nights. On top come final cleaning of €280, the local tax of €2.70 per person and night and a service fee of €25 per guest and stay. Example: 4 nights at the from-price for 10 people cost €2,290 in total, about €229 per person.

Is there a sauna or a fitness room?

From February 2027 there will be a sauna and a room for fitness, yoga or simply resting after the sauna. Ask us about the current status through the contact form.

Can you swim in the pond?

Swimming is possible at your own responsibility and risk. It is a natural pond with fish, and the water is not tested for bathing water quality.

Is fishing in the pond allowed?

Yes, fishing in the pond is allowed. It is a private natural pond with fish.

Is the house suitable for 10 people?

Yes. The whole house has 7 rooms, 3 bathrooms and 300 m². There is plenty of room for 10 guests.

Do we rent the whole house?

Yes, you rent the entire house and its grounds, with pond, garden and rowing boat. There are no other guests in the house or on the grounds.

Is Teichhof a self-catering house?

Yes. You cater for yourselves: the kitchen has a cooking island and an espresso machine, and everyone has a seat at the large dining table. The nearest supermarket is 6 km away, the nearest restaurant 330 m.

Does the house stand on its own?

Yes, Teichhof stands on its own at the edge of the woods. You are alone in the house and on the grounds of more than 20,000 m².

Which beds are there?

One king-size bed, four queen-size beds, two single beds and one queen-size sofa bed. The floor plan shows how the rooms are laid out.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, up to 3 pets. The charge is €59 per pet and stay. The entire grounds of more than 20,000 m² are fully fenced.

Is the house suitable for offsites, retreats and group meetings?

Yes. You rent the whole house with 7 rooms and 3 bathrooms, and there are no other guests in the house or on the grounds. Strategy offsites, yoga retreats, gatherings of friends and birthdays are a good fit: everyone has a seat at the large dining table, and Wi-Fi and a workspace are available. Weddings and parties are only possible when you book directly. Tell us what you are planning through the contact form.

Is breakfast included?

No, you cater for yourselves. The kitchen has a cooking island and an espresso machine. The nearest restaurant is 330 m away, the nearest supermarket 6 km.

How many parking spaces are there?

4 free parking spaces, one of them in the garage.

How does check-in work?

Self check-in with an electronic door lock, from 3 pm. Check-out is at 11 am.

Where is Teichhof?

Teichhof is at Steinparz 26, 3382 Schollach, Lower Austria, at the edge of the woods with a private pond. Melk Abbey is 8.6 km away, the A1 motorway 6.7 km, the nearest supermarket 6 km and the nearest restaurant 330 m. Directions
