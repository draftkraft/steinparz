By car

The A1 motorway is 6.7 km from the house. Coming from the west (Linz) take the Melk exit, from the east (Vienna) the Loosdorf exit.

There are 4 parking spaces at the house.

Arrival

Check-in is from 3 pm, check-out by 11 am.

You check in yourself with an electronic door lock, so a late arrival is no problem.

Nearby

The nearest restaurant is 330 m away, the nearest supermarket 6 km.

Melk Abbey is 8.6 km away.
