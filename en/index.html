Questions about Teichhof

Where is Teichhof?

Teichhof is at Steinparz 26, 3382 Schollach, Lower Austria, beside woodland and a private pond. Melk Abbey is 8.6 km away, the A1 motorway 6.7 km, the nearest supermarket 6 km and the nearest restaurant 330 m. How to get here

How many rooms and beds are there?

The house has seven rooms with one king-size bed, four queen-size beds, two single beds and a queen-size sofa bed, plus three bathrooms. It sleeps 10 guests. View the floor plan

Do we book the whole house?

Yes, you book the entire property. Teichhof includes a private pond, a rowing boat and a garden.

When are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3 pm and check-out is by 11 am. Self check-in uses an electronic door lock, so late arrivals are no problem.

Is there parking?

Yes. There are four free parking spaces on the property, one of them in a garage.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome, up to 3 per stay, for a fee of €59 per pet per stay. Please tell us about your pets when you book or send an enquiry.

Are the grounds fenced?

The entire spacious property is fenced. An additional fence around the garden separates it from the pond and prevents children from reaching the water directly from the garden.

Can we host a celebration?

Birthdays and family reunions are welcome. Weddings and parties are only possible with a direct booking: get in touch.

Is breakfast included?

No. Teichhof is self-catering, with a kitchen with a cooking island and an espresso machine. The nearest supermarket is 6 km away and the nearest restaurant 330 m. How to get here

How do I book?

Find availability and current prices in the booking section of this page. You can ask questions about your stay or a family gathering via the contact form.
