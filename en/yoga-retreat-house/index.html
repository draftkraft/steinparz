Questions about Teichhof

Can I rent Teichhof for a yoga retreat?

Yes. You rent the whole house with 7 rooms and 3 bathrooms and its grounds, with pond, garden and rowing boat. There are no other guests in the house or on the grounds. Tell us what you are planning through the contact form.

What does a yoga retreat at Teichhof cost?

The whole house costs from €413 per night, depending on the dates, with a minimum stay from 3 nights. On top come final cleaning of €280, the local tax of €2.70 per person and night and a service fee of €25 per guest and stay. Example: 4 nights at the from-price for 10 people cost €2,290 in total, about €229 per person.

What is the minimum stay?

Minimum stay: from 3 nights, depending on the dates. The booking calendar shows what applies to your dates.

How many participants does the house suit?

The house sleeps 10 guests, with 7 rooms and 3 bathrooms.

Where can we practise outdoors?

Your group can use the entire grounds of more than 20,000 m² and will find a fitting spot at any time of day: in the morning sun, in the evening light, in the shade under the oak, in the woods or by the water.

Is there a yoga room?

From February 2027 there will be a room for fitness, yoga or simply quiet, plus a sauna. Ask us about the current status through the contact form. The house already offers plenty of space: the kitchen with dining room and the living room have around 52 m² each, the gallery on the upper floor around 29 m².

Which beds are there?

One king-size bed, four queen-size beds, two single beds and one queen-size sofa bed. The floor plan shows how the rooms are laid out.

Is there catering?

No, you cater for yourselves. The kitchen has a cooking island and an espresso machine, and everyone has a seat at the large dining table. The nearest restaurant is 330 m away, the nearest supermarket 6 km.

How quiet is the location?

Teichhof lies at the edge of the woods with a private pond. You are alone in the house and on the grounds of more than 20,000 m².

Can you swim in the pond?

Swimming is possible at your own responsibility and risk. It is a natural pond with fish, and the water is not tested for bathing water quality.

Is fishing in the pond allowed?

Yes, fishing in the pond is allowed. It is a private natural pond with fish.

Is the house also suitable for other groups and offsites?

Yes. You rent the whole house with 7 rooms and 3 bathrooms, and there are no other guests in the house or on the grounds. Strategy offsites, yoga retreats, gatherings of friends and birthdays are a good fit: everyone has a seat at the large dining table, and Wi-Fi and a workspace are available. Weddings and parties are only possible when you book directly. Tell us what you are planning through the contact form.

How does check-in work?

Self check-in with an electronic door lock, from 3 pm. Check-out is at 11 am.

Where is Teichhof?

Teichhof is at Steinparz 26, 3382 Schollach, Lower Austria, at the edge of the woods with a private pond. Melk Abbey is 8.6 km away, the A1 motorway 6.7 km, the nearest supermarket 6 km and the nearest restaurant 330 m. Directions
